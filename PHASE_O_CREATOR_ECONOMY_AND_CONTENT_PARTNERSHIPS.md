## 🎯 Phase O Overview

Phase O establishes a comprehensive creator economy and partnership ecosystem, enabling chess content creators, coaches, and streaming personalities to build sustainable incomes through the platform while deepening integration with the global chess community. This phase transforms Chess Tactics Master from a standalone app into a platform for chess education and entertainment.

**Key Objectives**:
1. ✅ **Creator Tools** - Puzzle creation, coaching, content management platforms
2. ✅ **Monetization** - Revenue sharing models for creators, coaches, tournament organizers
3. ✅ **Coaching Platform** - Professional coach directory, lesson booking, payment processing
4. ✅ **Content Partnerships** - Chess.com, Lichess, YouTube, Twitch integrations
5. ✅ **Streaming Integration** - OBS integration, sponsorship tools, audience engagement
6. ✅ **Community Partnerships** - Chess federation partnerships, regional clubs, corporate sponsorships
7. ✅ **Brand Partnerships** - Sponsor tiers, co-branded content, exclusive features
8. ✅ **Content Distribution** - Puzzle libraries, educational content, famous games syndication
