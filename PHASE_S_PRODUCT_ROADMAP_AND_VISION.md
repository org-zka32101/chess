### 3. Feature Prioritization Framework ✅

**Decision Criteria** (weighted scoring):

| Criterion | Weight | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **User Impact** | 30% | How many users benefit? How much does it improve their experience? |
| **Business Impact** | 25% | Revenue opportunity? Market share? Competitive advantage? |
| **Strategic Fit** | 20% | Aligned with vision? Builds platform moat? Long-term value? |
| **Technical Effort** | 15% | How complex? How long to build? Maintenance burden? |
| **Market Timing** | 10% | Is market ready? Competitive pressure? Regulatory timing? |

**Scoring Matrix**:
- 9-10: Must-build now (strategic + user + business)
- 7-8: High priority (strong on 2+ dimensions)
- 5-6: Medium priority (good on 1-2 dimensions, acceptable effort)
- 3-4: Low priority (niche use case or high effort)
- 1-2: Deprioritize (low impact, high effort)

**Quarterly Prioritization Process**:
1. **Backlog grooming** (Week 1): Product team scores features, aligns with strategy
2. **Stakeholder review** (Week 2): Engineering, marketing, analytics input
3. **Leadership alignment** (Week 3): CEO/CFO approve quarterly roadmap
4. **Kickoff** (Week 4): Communicate to team, begin sprint planning

**Roadmap Flexibility**:
- 80% planned features (strategic, tested, high confidence)
- 20% flexible (market response, competitive pressure, user feedback)
- Quarterly review: Adjust roadmap based on progress and market changes
- Speed to market: Fast feature iteration (weekly releases)
