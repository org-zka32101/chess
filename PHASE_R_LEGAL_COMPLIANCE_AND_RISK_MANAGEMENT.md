### 6. Regulatory Compliance & Gaming Laws ✅

**Skill vs. Gambling Classification**:

**Chess Classification**: Skill game (not gambling) in most jurisdictions
- Legal basis: Chess is 99% skill, 1% luck (established in court precedent)
- Regulation: Most jurisdictions exempt skill games from gambling licensing

**Regional Compliance**:

**United States**:
- No federal gambling license required (skill game exemption)
- Unlawful Internet Gambling Enforcement Act (UIGEA): Not applicable (skill)
- State laws: Check state-specific skill game regulations (varies)
- Possible restrictions: Arizona, Arkansas, Georgia (verify current laws)

**European Union**:
- No unified EU gaming license needed (skill game)
- National regulations: Some countries require gaming licenses for any monetary games
- Compliance strategy: License in Ireland (permissive), establish local presence per country
- Germany: May require gaming license (consult local counsel)

**China**:
- Game license: Required through CADPA (China Audio-video Association)
- Monetary games: Not permitted (cannot charge for chess puzzles)
- Strategy: Operate as free app with premium features (not games of chance)

**India**:
- Skill games: Legal and exempt from gambling regulations
- Monetary tournaments: Legal (established in court precedent)
- Regulation: Only DPDP Act compliance required (not gambling-specific)

**Brazil**:
- Skill games: Legal and regulated
- Gaming authority: SECAP (Secretary of Social Programs)
- Regulation: Must comply with Brazilian Gaming Law
- License: May require to operate (consult local counsel)

---

**Tournament & Prize Pool Compliance**:

- **Prize pools**: Legal in all operating jurisdictions (chess is skill)
- **Taxation**: Prize winnings taxable to winners (1099 in US, T1099 equivalent elsewhere)
- **Fraud prevention**: Verify tournament eligibility, prevent collusion
- **Payout**: Winners receive prize minus taxes/fees (clear disclosure required)
